The long way north, and back again

A road-trip plan for João Paulo and Ineke: volcanic lakes, high desert, the Columbia River Gorge and a few days in Ellensburg before Seattle, with every Siskiyou Pass crossing timed for noon.

The one hard ruleCross the Siskiyou Pass at 12:00 noon

The summit is the highest point on Interstate 5, at 4,310 ft, just north of the California line. Crossing at midday gives the warmest, driest road and the best chance of avoiding morning frost and fog.

Fair warning: on clear autumn days the very warmest hour on the pass is often a bit after noon, around 1–3 pm, so noon is a safe, conservative target. Ice on the grade is most likely before mid-morning, so a noon crossing still leaves the risky hours behind you. Check current Siskiyou Summit conditions and TripCheck the night before. Traction requirements on the grade become common from November onward.
