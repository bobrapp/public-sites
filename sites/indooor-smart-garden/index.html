Notes on methodology

Rise upfront price reflects a fully‑outfitted 1‑level configuration commonly seen at retail (~$1,020). Base Rise 1‑Level can be found at $799 directly. Gardyn Home 4.0 is shown at its current $899 list price; promotional pricing as low as $799 has been observed.

Energy figures: Rise 1‑Level draws 75 W (lights on) / 15 W (lights off) on a 16/8 cycle ≈ 43 kWh/mo per the manufacturer FAQ; we use 51 kWh/mo as a conservative real‑world estimate including pump cycles. Gardyn 4.0 is shown at 47 kWh/mo (manufacturer + reviewer estimates).

Reliability score is a 0–100 composite weighted from public review aggregates, BBB complaint patterns, and warranty terms. It is an editorial heuristic, not a controlled study.
